Quick Facts

📍 Main Campus

Indang, Cavite, with satellite campuses across the province.

📜 History

Began in 1906 as an intermediate school and became a state university in 1998.

🏛️ Type

A public, state-run university offering undergraduate and graduate programs.

🌟 Core Values

Truth, Excellence, and Service.

🏫 Main Campus

About 70 hectares in Bancod, Indang, roughly 60 km southwest of Manila. It hosts the university’s main offices.

🏆 In the News

100% overall rating in the October 2026 Electronics Technicians exam, and 73rd of 130 Philippine institutions in UI GreenMetric 2026.
